---
title: Groups
description: Make a group for your team, manage who is in it, share projects with it, and let it own projects that outlive any one person.
---

A group is people who work together. Share a project with a group and everyone
in it has access. Let a group own a project and it does not depend on any one
person staying.

**Groups** in the header lists **Yours** and **Everyone's**, with how many
people and projects each has, and whether you are a **Manager** or **Member**.

## Making a group

Choose **New group**, give it a name and, optionally, **What it's for** — shown to
people choosing a group to share with. Choose **Create group**. You become its
manager.

## Managers and members

| | Can |
|---|---|
| **Manager** | Add and remove people, make others managers, rename and delete the group, and act as owner of the projects it owns. |
| **Member** | Have whatever access the group has. |

On the group's page, under **People**, a manager types a username in **Add a
person** and chooses **Add**, or **Add several…** to paste a list of usernames or
email addresses. Someone who has not signed in yet can be added by username.
Each person's **⋯** has **Make manager**, **Make member** and **Remove from
group**. A group always keeps one manager.

## Groups from your sign-in

A group marked **From sign-in** comes from your organization's identity provider.
Its members change there, and arrive in Garth when each person next signs in.

A group marked **Org-wide** belongs to the organization: administrators manage it.

## Projects a group owns

On a project you own, [transfer it](../../projects/settings/#ownership) to a
group you manage. From then on the group's managers act as its owner: they
change access, settings, transfer and delete it.

The group's page lists, under **Projects**, what it owns or has been given.

## What a group gives its members

Administrators can give a group a default model and per-person budgets. They
show under **For members** on the group's page. They apply to members who have
nothing set of their own; someone in several groups gets the largest budget.
Some models are offered only to members of chosen groups.

## Deleting a group

**⋯ → Delete group…**, then type its name. A group that owns projects cannot be
deleted: transfer them first. Its members lose the access it gave them.
