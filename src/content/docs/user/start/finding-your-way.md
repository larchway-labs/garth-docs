The header has five pages. **Admin** appears only for administrators.

| Page | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Projects** | Which projects you have, and how. Start here. |
| **Sessions** | Your sessions on every project — live, paused and ended. |
| **Groups** | The teams you are in, and the projects they own or share. |
| **Usage** | What your sessions have spent, against your budgets. |

## Projects

The list is split by how you have each project:

- **Yours** — projects you own.
- **Your groups** — projects a group you are in owns or was given. Pick one
  group from the menu beside it.
- **Shared with you** — projects someone shared with you directly. A share with
  an end date says when it ends.

Star a project to keep it under **Favorites** at the top. Without favorites, a
long list shows your **Recently opened** projects there instead. **Filter
projects…** searches names, owners and repository addresses. Archived projects
are under **Archived** at the end of the toolbar.

Administrators also see **All projects** — every project on the install. They
open a project they have no access to as a viewer.

## Your profile

Choose your avatar, top right, then **Your profile**. It has your name and
email, your git identity, your time zone, your default model and personal
instructions, your git host connections and where you are signed in. See
[Your profile](../../account/your-profile/).

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows your notifications, such as a
[spending alert](../../account/usage/#alerts) you set. Choose **Mark all read**
to clear the count. **Your alerts** links to where you set them.

## Command palette

Press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux), or choose **Search** in the header.
Type a few letters of a project or a page and press Enter. **New project…** is
there too.

## Banners

- An **announcement** from your administrators can appear above the header.
  Choose the cross to hide it for this visit.
- **Can't reach this install.** means your browser lost contact with Garth.
  What is on screen may be out of date; it checks again every few seconds, or
  choose **Retry**.
