---
title: Opening a project from Toft
description: Open in Garth on a Toft app's page takes you to the app's project and starts your session, or offers to link or import it.
---

On Toft, an app's owners and editors see **Open in Garth** on the app's page,
once your administrators have connected the two. It brings you to **Open from
Toft** in Garth, with the app's name, its install and its repository.

Following the link changes nothing by itself. Garth shows what it found, and you
choose.

## What Garth offers

| Garth found | You can |
|---|---|
| **The app's project** — a project linked to the app | **Start a session**. If you already have one there, it resumes. |
| **A project on the same repository**, not linked to the app | **Link the app and start a session** (if you own the project), or **Start a session without linking**. |
| **Nothing** — no project has the app or its repository | **Import and start a session**: a new project of yours from the repository, linked to the app. Give it a **Project name** first. |

## When it cannot open

- **The app's project is one you can read but not work on.** Its owner can give
  you access.
- **This app is linked to a project you can't open.** Ask the app's owner to
  share the project with you in Garth.
- **Nothing to open yet** — Garth cannot import the repository, and says why
  (for example, it is not from an allowed source). A project's owner can link the
  app instead, under **Settings → Toft apps**.
- **This link is incomplete** or **can't be opened** — use **Open in Garth** on
  the app's page again.

To link an app yourself, see [Project settings](../../projects/settings/#toft-apps).
