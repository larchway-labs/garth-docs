## New

- **Filters on Admin → Activity.** Filter by kind of event: sessions, projects and git, sign-ins, Toft, sandbox and network, settings and models, or people and groups. You can show only Garth's own actions or hide them, and pick a quick date range. The search covers all 90 days, not just the events on screen, and Load older keeps going. The filters are part of the page's address, so you can bookmark or share a view.
- **↑ brings back earlier messages.** In the session's message box, ↑ on the first line steps back through what you sent before, newest first. ↓ returns to what you were typing.

## Changed

- **The agent carries on after a Toft sign-in.** If the agent's `toft` command was refused because the session wasn't signed in to Toft, and you then sign it in from the Toft tab, Garth tells the agent. It continues without waiting to be asked.
- **Garth learns its public address sooner.** An administrator who is already signed in teaches Garth its address on their next secure request, rather than on their next sign-in.
