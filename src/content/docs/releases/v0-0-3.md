title: Garth 0.0.3
description: "November 11, 2026. A Sessions page for everything you have going, a clear message when the model ends a turn with nothing, and the database's size on Admin → System."
---

## New

- **Sessions, in the top bar.** Your sessions that are still going, on every project. Open one in a click, or end it from its menu. Below them are your most recent ended sessions, with their transcripts.

## Changed

- **Admin → System shows the database's size** in GB, to two decimal places.

## Fixed

- **A turn that ends with no reply says so.** A model can finish a turn with no text and no tool call, and the page used to go quiet. It now says that the model ended its turn without replying, and suggests resending or switching models.
